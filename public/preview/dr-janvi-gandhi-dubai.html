Parents ask

Common questions

When should my child first see a dentist?

Paediatric dental associations recommend a first visit when the first tooth appears, or by your child's first birthday. Early visits are short and gentle, and they help prevent problems before they start.

My child is scared of the dentist. Can you help?

Yes — this is what paediatric dentistry is for. Visits are paced to your child, everything is explained before it happens, and sedation options are available when a child needs extra support.

Why do baby teeth matter if they fall out anyway?

Baby teeth help children eat, speak and smile with confidence, and they hold space for the adult teeth. Decay in baby teeth can cause pain and infection, so they deserve care too.

What is myofunctional therapy?

Simple, guided exercises for the tongue, lips and face that improve breathing, swallowing and tongue posture — habits that influence how the jaws and teeth develop.
